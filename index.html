"Bringing deep learning to the deep spectrum"

Hello and welcome!

I'm Aditya Dixit, a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Indore. My research lies at the interaction of Remote Sensing, Hyperspectral Imaging, Computer Vision, and Deep Learning, with a focus on developing AI-driven frameworks for Environmental monitoring and Earth observation applications. I am fortunate to work under the guidance of Dr. Puneet Gupta.

Before joining IIT Indore, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Government Engineering College Jhalawar, Rajasthan. Over the years, I have developed a strong passion for research-oriented problem solving and interdisciplinary collaboration. I enjoy exploring challenging research problems, designing innovative AI solutions, and contributing to impactful applications in remote sensing and environmental science. Beyond research, I actively engage with the scientific community through collaborations, reviewing activities, mentoring students, and academic discussions. Outside academia, I enjoy travelling, playing chess, following cricket, reading, and listening to music.

Thank you for visiting my website. I am always open to research collaborations, academic discussions, and opportunities to work on exciting research problems in AI, remote sensing, and Earth observation.
